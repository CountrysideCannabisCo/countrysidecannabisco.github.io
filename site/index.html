Our position

Canadian cultivation is an origin worth putting on a label — but only if what arrives matches what was agreed.

We are built around that second part.

What we do

One producer, one licence, one point of accountability

We grow indoor flower and extract it ourselves across two facilities in St. Thomas, Ontario. Cultivation, extraction and finishing stay in-house, under a single Canadian company.

For a buyer — here or overseas — that is the point. One counterparty, one process to audit, and a batch that matches the one before it. We are GMP certified under a Drug Establishment Licence.
