Where we came from

Rooted in St. Thomas

Countryside is from St. Thomas, Ontario, in the farm country our name comes from. It is where we grow, where we extract and where we finish, all under one licence.

100 acres · Ontario
The farm

One hundred acres of Ontario farmland

Our farm is the countryside in Countryside: one hundred acres of open Ontario land, and the room to keep growing.

The indoor grow

Grown indoors, room by room

Our indoor grow runs room by room, with light, climate and feed set rather than left to the weather. One harvest matches the next, whatever the season is doing outside.

What we do

Grow, extract, finish

We grow, extract and finish cannabis ourselves, and put that work to use for other businesses: bulk flower and extracts, contract manufacturing, tolling and white label, for partners in Canada and overseas.

At home, the same floor makes six brands on Canadian shelves: Harvest Reserve, Shatterizer Extracts, Greazy, Ellevia, Litti and Buddy Blooms.
